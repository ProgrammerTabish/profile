Großry

Get wholesale prices without having to buy wholesale quantities. Students pool their monthly grocery demand, Großry buys in bulk once, and a student Order Runner brings it to campus.

Try this path

  1. Register with a student email (the code is shown on screen) or use the demo account.
  2. Shop: open a product, watch the bulk-price progress bar move as you add packs.
  3. Orders: check the cost breakdown and savings ledger, then submit.
  4. Press Advance ▶ above to move through the monthly cycle: demand lock, procurement, arrival, sorting, distribution.
  5. Run: volunteer as Order Runner or backup, then scan handovers on distribution day.
  6. Switch to Admin for procurement, inventory, finance and the anonymised insights preview.

The model

Student demand→Aggregation→Bulk threshold→Procurement→Order Runner→Sorting→Slot allocation→QR handover→Transparent savings

Design principles

  • Savings are measured against a stated reference basket, never an arbitrary price.
  • Großry takes 20% of the verified saving, and the cart shows it.
  • No single point of failure: every run has a backup runner.
  • Data minimisation: no photo needed, no individual data sold. Only aggregated, thresholded statistics.
  • Chilled and frozen goods stay out of student-run distribution until food-hygiene handling is reviewed.
