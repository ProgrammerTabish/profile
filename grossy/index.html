Großry

Wholesale prices without wholesale quantities. Students pool their monthly groceries, buy in bulk once, and save around 20%.

Try it

  1. Use the demo account.
  2. Shop: watch your saving grow as you add items.
  3. Orders: submit, then press Advance ▶ to move the month forward to pick-up day.
  4. Run: earn grocery credit as Runner or Distributor.

Switch to Admin for the operator's view. Terms and Privacy in the app are drafts.
